Why this works

Better than a textbook, because you're in it

Reading about the bullwhip effect and causing it yourself are two different kinds of understanding. This gives you the second one.

See the bullwhip effect happen

Make a forecasting or ordering mistake. Watch demand and inventory amplify through the network in real time — the same P&G Pampers phenomenon that costs real companies millions. Then understand why it happened.

Make expensive mistakes before they cost you money

Over-order. Under-forecast. Single-source a critical supplier. Experience the consequences safely — then carry the intuition into a real job before it costs you one.

Train for the disruptions companies actually face

Supplier failure. Demand spikes. Lead-time shocks. Inventory shortages. Cash pressure. Scenarios drawn from real supply chain crises, not hypotheticals.

What is the bullwhip effect?

A supply chain phenomenon where small fluctuations in consumer demand amplify dramatically upstream — a 5% demand increase at retail can translate to 40%+ overordering at the manufacturing level. You experience it directly in Chapter 2.

Is this useful for APICS CSCP preparation?

Yes, as a practice supplement. The game maps to all three CSCP domains (SCPE, SCD, SCIBP), and each chapter ends with an exam-ready flashcard covering a key concept — MAPE, bullwhip effect, safety stock formulas, TCO, SCRM, and more. Supply Chain Disaster is an independent learning tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of ASCM/APICS.

How long does it take to complete?

About 10–15 minutes per chapter. The full 10-chapter campaign takes approximately 2.5 hours. You can save and resume at any time.

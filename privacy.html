Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Supply Chain Disaster ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Supply Chain Disaster (the "Game" or "Service") at supplychaindisaster.com. By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Email address — provided when you turn on cloud saves, restore a purchase, or make a purchase. We email you a one-time code to confirm the address is yours; it's also used for your purchase confirmation. For cloud saves we don't store the address itself as your account: we keep a keyed hash of it (to find your account when you verify again), a masked version for display (for example a••••@example.com) and an internal account ID that your saved campaigns are filed under.
  • Saved campaigns (cloud saves) — if you turn on cloud saves, the campaigns you play while signed in are copied to our server: your game state (decisions, results, cash, stock, scenario order, concept scores) plus progress details such as chapter, quarter and when it was last saved. They're only readable and changeable with your signed-in session, never by email address alone. We keep the latest version and one earlier recovery copy of each campaign.
  • Order ID — generated by our payment processor (Lemon Squeezy) when you purchase the Core Game Pass (or purchased Full Access earlier). Used solely to verify and restore your access.
  • Contact form submissions — name, email, and message you send through our contact form. Used to respond to your enquiry.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage analytics — via Google Analytics 4 (GA4). We collect anonymised event data such as page views, game events (such as pregame_view, industry_select, start_click, game_start, run_started, turn_completed, chapter_started, chapter_completed, resume_started, cloud_save_prompt_shown, cloud_sync_succeeded, local_save_failed, free_scenario_complete, core_offer_view, checkout_start, purchase_confirmed and paid_campaign_open, with the industry, chapter and quarter involved and a random campaign ID that isn't linked to your email), and device/browser type. No email address, account ID, order ID, payment detail or game save is sent to analytics. IP addresses are anonymised. You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Local storage — your campaigns (each saved separately, updated automatically as you play, with one earlier recovery copy) and your access status (a signed access token issued by our server after a verified purchase or restore) are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device unless you turn on cloud saves (see above) or use "Restore a purchase", which sends the email you enter to our server so we can email you a one-time code (via our email provider, Resend) and look up purchases made with that email at Lemon Squeezy.

1.3 Payment Information

We do not collect or store payment card details. All transactions are handled by Lemon Squeezy. Their privacy policy governs the handling of your payment data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and restore your premium access across devices.
  • To keep your saved campaigns available on your other devices when you turn on cloud saves.
  • To send transactional emails (purchase confirmation, one-time sign-in codes). Turning on cloud saves does not sign you up for marketing or reminder emails.
  • To respond to contact form enquiries.
  • To measure and improve game performance and conversion through anonymised analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

  • Cloud saves: each saved campaign is kept for 12 months after it was last synced, then deleted automatically. You can delete a campaign from the cloud at any time on the /play page. One-time codes expire after 10 minutes; sign-in sessions last 30 days from your last visit.
  • Old email backups made with the previous "back up to email" option are kept until they expire (180 days after they were last saved) and can only be retrieved by verifying that email address.
  • Email addresses collected for purchases are retained for as long as needed to provide your access or as required to fulfil our legal obligations.
  • Analytics data is retained per Google's standard GA4 retention settings (default: 14 months).
  • LocalStorage data remains on your device until you clear your browser data or explicitly delete it.

5. Cookies

The Game uses Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to distinguish users and sessions for analytics. These are first-party cookies set on the supplychaindisaster.com domain. If you turn on cloud saves, a strictly necessary sign-in cookie (scd_sess) keeps you signed in for up to 30 days; it is HttpOnly, sent only to our own servers, and removed when you sign out. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are set. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though this may affect analytics functionality.

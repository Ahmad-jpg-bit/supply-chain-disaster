## Statistics audit

- **45% of one year's profit (disruptions).** Found only in the `digital-automation-supply-chain` metadata (and the stale `blog.html` copy); its body never supported it and it was an unqualified "now cost… up to". Removed, not restated. For reference: secondary sources cite McKinsey Global Institute (Aug 2020) as 42% (exhibit) and 45% (scenario summary) of one year's **EBITDA**, expected losses averaged over a **decade**, disruptions of a month or longer every 3.7 years. We could not open the report (see unresolved 3), so no figure was reinstated.
- **6% vs 13% visibility.** Different surveys: QIMA 2025, self-reported "full visibility into sourcing networks, including raw materials" (13%); a GEODIS Supply Chain Worldwide Survey, cited via an aggregator, "full end-to-end" (6%; year not stated, file name suggests 2020). Now presented as separate, dated, differently defined measures in `real-time-monitoring-supply-chain`, `information-technology-supply-chain`, `supply-chain-visibility-signs` and `supply-chain-data-literacy-signs`.
- **Other changes.** Retracted paper removed (above). "More than 85% lack traceability" re-attributed to a 2020 Bain release. PwC 96%/53% removed (page blocked). McKinsey 42% tier-two figure removed (secondary reports conflict).
- **Site-wide.** The shared CTA notes said "Chapters 1–3 free" on 49 pages and "All 10 chapters always free" on 47; now "FMCG Chapter 1 free" (+ Core Game Pass price where space allowed). Four articles presented Supply Chain Disaster as a hazard-intelligence platform; corrected. Raw Markdown stripped from description metadata (`**`, `[text](url)`) on four more pages.
